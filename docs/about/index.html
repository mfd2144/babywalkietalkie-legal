About BabyWalkieTalkie

Always close, always safe.

BabyWalkieTalkie turns two phones into a live audio baby monitor. One phone stays next to your baby, and the other goes with you.

How it works

  • Pair once. The parent phone shows a 6-digit code or QR code, and the baby phone scans or enters it. The two devices stay linked until you unlink them.
  • Listen live. Audio from the baby phone is streamed to the parent phone in real time. You can also talk back, like a walkie-talkie.
  • Cry alerts. The baby phone watches the sound level on the device itself. When it passes the sensitivity you set, the parent phone gets a notification, even when the app is closed.

Your privacy

Audio is streamed live and is never recorded or stored. Cry detection works from the volume level on the device and does not analyse or upload what is said. The Privacy Policy has the details.

BabyWalkieTalkie is a convenience tool. It is not a medical or safety device and does not replace direct adult supervision of a child. Never leave a child unattended relying solely on the app. See the Terms of Service.
