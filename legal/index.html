Legal Consent

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This page sums up what you agree to when you create an account and use BabyWalkieTalkie. The full terms are in the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, and those documents take precedence.

By using the app, you confirm that:

  • you are at least 18 years old;
  • you have read and accept the Terms of Service;
  • you have read the Privacy Policy and understand how your data is processed;
  • you will use the app only to monitor a child in your care, or with the consent of the child's parent or legal guardian;
  • you understand that the app is not a medical device and does not replace direct adult supervision of a child;
  • you understand that audio and alerts can be delayed or interrupted by network, device or service problems.

Permissions you grant

  • Microphone: you allow the baby device to stream live audio to your paired parent device and to measure the sound level for cry detection. Audio is not recorded or stored.
  • Camera: you allow the app to scan the pairing QR code. No images are saved.
  • Notifications: you allow the app to send cry alerts to the parent device.

Processing of personal data

You consent to the processing of your account, pairing, notification and diagnostics data as described in the Privacy Policy. This includes transfer to service providers (Google Firebase and Agora) that may store data outside your country.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time. Turn off microphone, camera or notification access in your phone's settings, unlink your devices in the app, or email m.f.dogan84@gmail.com to have your account and data deleted. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before, but some features may stop working.
