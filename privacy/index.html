Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what data the BabyWalkieTalkie mobile app ("the app") collects, why, and what control you have over it. The app is developed and operated by Mehmet Fatih Doğan, an individual developer ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the personal data described here.

In short: audio is streamed live between your paired devices and is never recorded or stored. We keep only what is needed to sign you in, pair your devices and send cry alerts.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, display name and a unique user ID. These come from email sign-up, Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your real email address.
  • Pairing data: temporary 6-digit pairing codes, the ID of the channel that links your two devices, which device is the parent and which is the baby, and whether each device is currently online.
  • Notification tokens: a push-notification token for each of your devices, used to send cry alerts.
  • Usage and diagnostics: app usage events, crash reports, device model, operating system version and app version, collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Settings stored on your device: preferences such as cry-detection sensitivity. These stay on your phone.

2. Microphone and camera

  • Microphone: used to stream live audio between your paired devices and to measure the sound level for cry detection. Audio is transmitted in real time and is not recorded, stored or analysed for content by us or our providers. Cry detection compares the volume level against your sensitivity setting on the device itself.
  • Camera: used only to scan the pairing QR code. No photos or video are saved or uploaded.

You can turn off either permission at any time in your phone's settings. The features that need it will then stop working.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To pair your devices and connect them for live audio.
  • To send cry alerts to the parent device.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve the app.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or build advertising profiles.

4. Legal basis

We process account, pairing and notification data because it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract). We process diagnostics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and secure. We use microphone and camera access only with your explicit permission, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Service providers

We rely on these providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics): accounts, pairing data, notifications and diagnostics. Firebase privacy
  • Agora: real-time audio transport between paired devices. Agora privacy policy
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose those options, and push-notification delivery.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • Pairing codes are single-use. Channel data is removed when you unlink your devices.
  • Notification tokens are removed when you sign out.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept according to Firebase's retention settings, typically up to 14 months for analytics and 90 days for crash reports.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU/UK GDPR and Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy,
  • correct inaccurate data,
  • have your data and account deleted,
  • object to or restrict certain processing,
  • withdraw consent at any time,
  • lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

To exercise any of these rights, including deleting your account and all related data, email m.f.dogan84@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

The app is meant for parents and caregivers aged 18 or over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. A child's voice may be heard through the live audio stream, but it is never recorded or stored.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to your pairing channel is limited to your two paired devices, which authenticate with short-lived tokens. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
